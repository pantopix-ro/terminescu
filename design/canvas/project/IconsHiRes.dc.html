Tab icons · 12 x 12

26 icons for tab identities, drawn on a 12 x 12 grid instead of 8 x 8. A new tab takes a random icon that no other tab in the window uses yet; the side tab row shows its first tab's icon until it becomes a group. Drawn at 12 px, one grid cell is one point, so each pixel lands on two device pixels on a Retina screen and stays crisp.
